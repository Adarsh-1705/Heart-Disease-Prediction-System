💡 Wellness

Heart Health Tips

🥗

Eat Heart-Healthy

Favor fruits, vegetables, whole grains. Limit saturated fats, sodium, and added sugars to reduce cholesterol.

🏃

Stay Active

Aim for 150 minutes of moderate aerobic exercise weekly. Even brisk walking can cut heart disease risk by 35%.

🚭

Quit Smoking

Smoking doubles your risk of heart disease. Quitting within 1 year cuts the risk to half that of a smoker.

😴

Quality Sleep

7–9 hours of quality sleep per night. Poor sleep is linked to high blood pressure and inflammation.

🧘

Manage Stress

Chronic stress raises cortisol and blood pressure. Try meditation, yoga, or deep breathing daily.

💊

Monitor BP & Cholesterol

Check blood pressure monthly. Target below 120/80 mmHg. High BP is the #1 controllable risk factor.

🍷

Limit Alcohol

Excess alcohol raises triglycerides and blood pressure. Limit to ≤1 drink/day for women, ≤2 for men.

🩺

Regular Checkups

Annual cardiac screenings catch silent issues early. Early detection is the most effective treatment.
